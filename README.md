# 📊Customer-Churn-Analysis-Telco-Customer-Churn-Dataset
This project analyzes telecom customer churn to understand why customers leave and which factors influence churn. Using Python, Pandas, Seaborn and Matplotlib.The analysis focuses on contract type, tenure, charges, gender, senior citizens, and internet service to help businesses reduce churn and improve customer retention.

Customer retention is one of the biggest challenges for subscription-based companies.  
This project analyzes **why customers leave (Churn)** and identifies patterns to help businesses reduce churn and retain customers.

---

## 🎯 Project Goal
To analyze customer behavior and find:
- Who churns more?
- Which customers are at highest risk?
- What factors influence churn the most?
- What business actions can reduce churn?

## 🧠 Key Insights
- Customers with **Month-to-Month contracts churn the most**
- **Low tenure customers** are more likely to leave
- Customers with **high monthly charges** churn more
- **Fiber optic users** churn more compared to DSL users
- Gender does **not** significantly affect churn

---

## 🏢 Business Recommendations
1️⃣ Convert Month-to-Month users to **Yearly Contracts**  
2️⃣ Offer **discounts / loyalty benefits** to new customers  
3️⃣ Create **lower cost plans** for high charge customers  
4️⃣ Improve **Fiber Internet service + support**  
5️⃣ Build **retention strategy** for first-year customers
